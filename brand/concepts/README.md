# Three ways to bridge

- **A · Parallel span** ([icon-a.png](icon-a.png)): two arches and one deck make parallel worktrees feel connected and steady.
- **B · Confluence** ([icon-b.png](icon-b.png)): three separate lanes rise into a shared span, making many worktrees feel reachable through one calm tool.
- **C · Joined planes** ([icon-c.png](icon-c.png)): one arch connects stacked glass planes, giving each worktree a distinct place in a shared structure.

## The pick: B

Three open lanes make parallel worktrees visible without a literal bridge illustration.  
The rising silhouette remains recognizable in one colour and transfers cleanly to a menu-bar glyph.  
The final charcoal tile gives the violet glass stronger separation at small sizes and on either desktop appearance.

The refined master is [../icon/setu-icon-1024.png](../icon/setu-icon-1024.png). It uses the same geometry, broader quiet highlights, cleaner lane gaps and a charcoal tile. The three concepts and the master are each 1024 × 1024 PNG.
